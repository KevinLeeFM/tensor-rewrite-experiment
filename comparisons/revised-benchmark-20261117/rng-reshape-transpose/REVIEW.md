# Independent review of RNG shape observations

**Two model cases, with different observations.** The transpose theorem compares the joint point mass for every complete Boolean rectangle. The reshape theorem preserves every row-major stream value for rectangles of equal cardinality under the declared same-stream coupling. Neither proves an RNG implementation produces independent draws, reuses a seed in this way, advances state identically, or respects a particular layout. Rectangles cover two-dimensional shapes and the transpose swaps the two axes. No arbitrary-rank claim. Uniform U32[0,2) is the source-facing finite example; nonuniform and singleton-support Boolean weights extend the mathematical model. No normal-distribution result. [Frozen calculations](RngSpec.lean) · [RNG contract](../../../sources/xla/docs/operation_semantics.md#L4422)

The mass takes a product of cell weights over both axes, and the inverse transpose sends an entire proposed output back to its unique input. exchange explicitly interchanges two finite products. total=(w(false)+w(true))^(rows×cols) is the normalization of the independent-draw model by finite distributivity; the theorem proves equality of numerators and denominators, not a separately formalized sum-over-all-arrays normalization identity. Its positive weight-sum guard makes both denominators positive, including 1 for an empty rectangle. Thus equality of all these pairs gives the same normalized probability for every point. Balanced one-cell marginals would be insufficient: the equal-probability horizontal-stripe array and its complement have balanced marginals, while neither is the transpose of the first horizontal stripe. The two support-exclusion controls test this correlation issue. [Proof](RngProof.lean) · [Controls](RngControls.lean)

For each admitted output cell the row-major address k is less than the common element count; oldCoordinatesValid proves its quotient/remainder by the old column extent is a valid input cell. The reshape theorem then reconstructs k for an arbitrary stream. An empty output has no indices. On the 2×2 deterministic transpose diagnostic, stream(k)=(k=1) gives different values at transposed versus unchanged off-diagonal addresses. It refutes a stronger same-stream assertion only. The pinned single-user RNG branches mutate shape; earlier reshape-empty and no-op guards or degenerate transpose can take precedence.
