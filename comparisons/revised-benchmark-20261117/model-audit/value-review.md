## Counting decisions

- The six word self-comparisons have one parameterized operator and one direction-to-constant replacement. Reversing the order of symmetric operands, reusing a helper in a caller, and deriving several equivalent characterizations add no case. Max versus Min and EQ/true versus NE/false have different operations. Each of the unsigned lower-bound laws has its own fixed comparison and constant result. The integral family already has abstract predecessors in OrderFamily.
- Scalar and nonempty heterogeneous tuple reductions have distinct original and replacement terms and types. The tuple proof supports coupled whole-state reducers. The two sort arities also differ in their typed output and observation. A dynamic descriptor admitting an unsorted runtime fiber is a different model from a static tensor with singleton or empty fibers. A rank-one unbounded Pad descriptor admits a wrong output length. These frozen runtime model refutations survive alongside the static successes.
- The counterexamples for duplicate-role Scatter, signed-index Gather, and hoisting both scales in a selected contraction disprove their original mathematical claims. The exact repaired boundaries are useful theorems, with evidence linked on the negative rows. Crediting those restrictions as separate newly proved rewrites would conceal why the original predicates fail. Boolean-to-binary32 multiplication has a separate, defined exceptional-class contract with a Lean proof of the negation of universal validity. Every stricter multiplication model respecting that necessary condition excludes the witness.
- The ordered-NaN certificates associated with HandleCompare/nonnegative-ge and nonnegative-le establish that a true answer is incompatible with an ordered comparison on the given bits. The Lean model does not evaluate the matched nonnegative producer or compute the recognizer. Historical strict evaluator witnesses for both orientations are preserved in [the independent recovery review](../recovered-witnesses/COMPARE-SCALAR-REVIEW.md). Neither certificate is counted as a standalone frozen producer-to-replacement model here.
- The tuple scheduling counterexample applies to specific before/after prerequisite graphs and the relative ordering of two effects. Its independently reviewed negative verdict is preserved in [the schedule review](../tuple-projection/order/REVIEW.md). Its result is not an additional shape/value counterexample to tuple projection. Similarly, dynamic-descriptor length classification alone is not a positive proof for all tensor values.
- The logical barrier law observes every legal projected user after compaction under the modeled keep rule. The sort theorem allows any fiber-local selection, which entails the modeled sort cases under the stated guard. The causal Select/NE theorem is uniform over all masks; its integer GE instance uses concrete one/zero arms. Source matcher priority and graph effects remain separately documented.
