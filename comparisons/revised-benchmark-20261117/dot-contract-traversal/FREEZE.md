# Moving a transpose of contracting axes to a constant

HandleDot/reorder-contracting-lhs and /reorder-contracting-rhs. Rank-two nonconstant F32 parameter [2,2] → transpose [1,0] → reshape [4]. Other operand F32 nonuniform literal [4]; no batch axes; contract the sole axis on both sides; scalar output. A single flatten drops both dimensions, no one-to-one unmodified axis. The helper moves only those contracting dimensions and inverts the reshape/transpose on the constant. The 2×2 transpose is self-inverse, so flip(k) = (k%2)*2+k/2 applies in both directions. When the constant was on the left the helper remaps its internal argument order and restores original operand roles. Layout insensitive; eligible normal-precision dot; earlier strength/association exits disabled or inapplicable. No matrix-by-matrix fast path. The model observes ascending contracting-index F32 RNE accumulation. Nonnegative integral inputs at most 2^24 and weights 0,1,2 give representable inputs and exact products, so fused vs separate product rounding is immaterial. Finite sums are below overflow. The original and transformed calculate exactly the terms in TraversalSpec, with operand-side multiplication explicit. Any different accumulation when permitted by backend semantics is outside this strict observation. No native execution.
