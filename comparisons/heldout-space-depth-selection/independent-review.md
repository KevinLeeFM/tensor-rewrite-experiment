## Gate findings

1. **Source conditions and domains:** The draft tracks the first split, suffix, chunk cap, selected-axis offset rule, scaled output extents, ordered slices, and mathematical reshape/transpose operators. The permutation condition indeed implies `t < s`, so concat axis `t` is legal and the output extent update `t-1` is distinct from split axis `s`. In Nat arithmetic the mismatch plus `x[s]=k*c` excludes `k=0`; no blanket nonempty tensor premise is needed. `c=0` and zero extents on other axes remain possible. `k=1` is source-compatible. The explicit int64 no-overflow profile and logical-operator semantics are acceptable disclosed model assumptions, but are not a C++ correspondence proof.

2. **Outer rank:** `m ≥ n` is a disclosed model restriction, not a source guard, and the inspected source provides no invariant proving it. The loop reads `outer_reshape->shape().dimensions(dim)` for `dim < input rank`, while `Shape::dimensions(int)` documents a valid-index precondition and indexes its dimensions span. Thus smaller `m` lies outside this mathematical model and may make source execution invalid/undefined; do not state full source-branch coverage. Keep the restriction explicit and label coverage as the `m ≥ n` partition unless a separately sourced invariant is established. Extra output dimensions beyond `n` can remain subject to reshape element-count equality.

3. **Typed frame transport (blocking):** Shape update commutation alone is insufficient. A minimal exact bridge must (a) identify each input-split-axis slice `Tensor (x[s↦c]) α` with the distinct concat frame whose fixed extents are `x` except at `t`, with concat extent `x[t]`; (b) transport every slice tensor by explicit bounded-coordinate casts along the dependent equality `x[s↦c] = frame.shape (x[t])`; (c) show that the ordered `k` transported blocks have the exact concat-axis extents and total shape `x[s↦c][t↦x[t]*k]`; and (d) prove pointwise that `Frame.concatenate` selects slice `i` at concat coordinate quotient/remainder `i` and the original split coordinate `i*c + local`, including the `c=0` and other-empty-axis cases. Then derive equality of that result's shape with the outer reshape input shape and use the actual reshape/transpose index maps to prove the same values at every legal output coordinate. None of these equalities or coordinate-selection facts may be premises or definitions that stipulate the final desired map. This is the smallest named obligation; a general shape-transport framework is not yet justified.

4. **Exact goal / proof readiness:** The prose is meaningful and does not assume `L=R`, but no Lean-elaborated goal pins the definitions, source guard, arithmetic profile, and dependent casts. The bridge is only shape arithmetic. Therefore there is no exact theorem to hash-freeze or evaluate for axiom use. The current artifact cannot pass pre-proof freeze.
