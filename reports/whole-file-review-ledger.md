# Whole-file discovery review ledger

The ledger is census/review-ledger.json, generated by tools/build_review_ledger.py.
It enumerates the normalized structural candidate calls and records their exact
source ranges, enclosing contexts, macro-view adjustments and related extracted
schemas/pilot theorems. All records are pending semantic review. Existing value
proofs do not promote a source packet to verified status.

The current discovery spans all 58 visitor handler records and 959 candidate calls:
317 mutable accessors needing alias/write analysis; 333 replacement/rewrite-helper
candidates; 212 construction candidates; 83 metadata/layout write candidates; and
14 other mutation/helper candidates. These disjoint name-based buckets organize
inspection; they do not establish effects or a number of distinct rewrite rules.
Factories and cloning calls are deliberately separated from actual graph effect
classification rather than counted automatically as transformations.

HandleAllReduce and HandleReduceScatter have no local calls matching this
discovery vocabulary. Their source delegates to HandleAllReduceOrReduceScatter;
they remain explicit handler review entries, rather than disappearing as empty
workloads. Other indirect/helper effects also require tracing.

Every candidate is accounted for once by its source span. The validator checks
that none was omitted/duplicated, source identities are unchanged and no pending
record claims an accepted source proof. It also detects extracted replacement
cases that lost their source link. Negative controls omit a packet, fabricate
source text and invent acceptance evidence; each must be rejected.

The ledger is scientific source-review evidence. Scheduling remains in notes/TODO.md.
It is not a frozen verification denominator or a completed first proof-attempt
sweep. Type/layout variants, earlier exits, macro propagation, called helpers,
actual observable effects and independently reviewed contracts remain open.
